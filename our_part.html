Our Part

Learn how you can protect yourself and others from falling victim to scams.

Be alert and take a second to think about clicking links or sending any personal details or money over. Always make sure to check the message/request via official means, don't share OTP/passwords and consult someone if anything seems suspicious. Report a scam immediately when it happens.

Keep yourself safe by being careful while handling strangers, unknown websites, and other proposals. Be sure about the authenticity of any information you have by cross-checking the source and avoiding hasty decisions to ensure that all your accounts remain safe.

🔎

Stay Informed

Share scam warnings with your family and friends, especially those who may be less familiar with online scams. A quick conversation can help someone avoid losing money or personal information.

📢

Report & Share

Report scam attempts to the authorities immediately to protect others from falling into similar traps.

☎️

Verify, Don't Trust

Always confirm unexpected interactions with official channels. Never rely only on messages, calls, or links provided by someone you do not know, without verifying with the official source.

📖

Keep Learning About Scams

Stay updated on common scam methods and warning signs. Knowing how scammers operate can help you recognise suspicious offers, messages, and websites before you become a victim.
